# SHADOW — Document Library & Templates

**Ready-to-Use Governance & Operational Documents**

---

## GOVERNANCE DOCUMENTS

### 1. SHADOW Charter (Founding Document)

**Status:** Final, reviewed by founders

**Key sections:**
- Organisational purpose
- Core principles (anonymity, autonomy, minimal centralisation)
- Membership criteria
- Member rights and responsibilities
- Project sovereignty principle
- Commons role and authority limits
- Conflict resolution framework
- Constitutional amendments procedure

**Length:** 8–12 pages
**Audience:** All members
**Review cycle:** Annually (Member Assembly at Stage 3+)

---

### 2. Membership Agreement

**Status:** Template ready for personalisation

```
SHADOW MEMBERSHIP AGREEMENT

1. ACCEPTANCE OF CHARTER
   Member accepts SHADOW Charter and all policies.

2. PSEUDONYM & IDENTITY
   - Member will be known in SHADOW by pseudonym: _______
   - Member's legal identity is verified and stored securely
   - Member's pseudonym is permanent and personally owned

3. RIGHTS
   - Right to participate in projects
   - Right to build reputation/portfolio
   - Right to request arbitration for disputes
   - Right to access own data
   - Right to leave SHADOW at any time

4. OBLIGATIONS
   - Treat others respectfully (Code of Conduct)
   - Fulfill project commitments
   - Respect confidentiality
   - Report serious misconduct
   - Keep account secure

5. PRIVACY
   - Legal identity disclosed only when legally required
   - Pseudonym may be public (member chooses visibility)
   - Personal data protected per privacy policy
   - Member may request data export/deletion

6. TERMINATION
   - Member may leave at any time
   - Projects' commitments remain binding
   - Portfolio data remains permanent record
   - Can reapply after departure

Accepted by: ________ | Date: ________ | SHADOW ID: ________
```

### 3. Team Leader Charter

**Purpose:** Defines Team Leader role and accountability

```
TEAM LEADER CHARTER

You are the Team Leader for Project: _____________

AUTHORITY (you decide):
- Project objective and scope
- Team recruitment/removal
- Role assignments
- Work prioritization
- Technical approaches
- Internal processes

RESPONSIBILITY (you are accountable for):
- Project delivery on schedule
- Fair treatment of team
- Accurate contribution records
- Budget management (if applicable)
- Security and confidentiality
- Legal compliance
- Honest communication
- Designating your successor

YOU CANNOT:
- Override SHADOW Charter or Core Rules
- Claim authority over other projects
- Prevent members from leaving
- Deny portfolio credit to retaliate
- Exploit or abuse team members
- Hide financial information

ACCOUNTABILITY:
If team member files complaint → Arbitration investigation
If misconduct found → Possible removal from Team Leader role
If serious breach → SHADOW membership suspension or removal

You understand and accept these terms.

Signed: ________ | Project: ________ | Date: ________
```

### 5. Privacy Policy (GDPR Compliant)

**Key sections:**
- What data collected (legal name, email, IP, activity logs)
- Why collected (legal compliance, security, operations)
- How stored (encrypted, secure servers)
- Who can access (Commons staff with need-to-know)
- Retention periods (1–7 years depending on type)
- Member rights (access, correction, deletion, portability)
- Breach procedures (notify within 72 hours)
- Third-party sharing (legal requirement only)

---

### 6. Information Classification Policy

```
INFORMATION CLASSIFICATION

Level 1: PUBLIC (share freely)
- Project names and descriptions
- General SHADOW announcements
- Member pseudonym and portfolio
- Governance decisions (high-level)
- Public reputation data

Level 2: INTERNAL (SHADOW members only)
- Member directory
- Project charter details
- Detailed decision logs
- Financial reports (summary)
- Governance records

Level 3: PROJECT-CONFIDENTIAL (project team only)
- Project client information
- Specific technical implementation details
- Sensitive business logic
- Interim drafts
- Internal project communications

Level 4: RESTRICTED (authorized only)
- Member legal identities
- Sensitive client data
- Financial details (project-level)
- Ongoing investigations
- Dispute details (while active)
- System credentials

VIOLATIONS:
- Accidental: Education + monitoring
- Deliberate: Investigation + arbitration
- Serious: Removal from SHADOW
```

## PROJECT DOCUMENTS

### 7. Project Charter Template

**~4-5 pages; Completion: 1–2 hours**

**Sections:**

1. **Leadership** — Team Leader, Successor
2. **Objective & Scope** — Problem, solution, success criteria
3. **Team & Roles** — Members, commitment types
4. **Deliverables** — What will exist at completion
5. **Intellectual Property** — Who owns work created
6. **Confidentiality Level** — Information classification
7. **Financial** — Funding source, budget, owner
8. **Decision-Making** — Who decides what
9. **Exit Terms** — How contributors leave
10. **Resources Needed** — Requests from Commons
11. **Legal** — Contracts, data protection, IP registration
12. **Signatures** — All parties agree

**When:** Before first team meeting
**Who:** Team Leader completes with Commons
**Storage:** Shared access (team + Commons)

---

### 8. Contributor Agreement Template

**~3 pages; Completion: 30 mins per contributor**

**Sections:**

1. **Commitment Type** — Volunteer/Hourly/Fixed-fee/Revenue-share/Equity
2. **Duration & Availability** — Start/end dates, time commitment
3. **Role & Responsibilities** — What they'll deliver, success criteria
4. **Compensation** — Amount, schedule, currency, conditions
5. **IP Ownership** — Pre-existing IP, work created, attribution
6. **Confidentiality** — Classification, sharing restrictions
7. **Exit Terms** — Notice, final deliverables, payment
8. **Dispute Resolution** — How disagreements are handled
9. **Mutual Expectations** — Commitments from both sides
10. **Termination** — Early exit procedures, work-in-progress handling
11. **Signatures** — Both parties agree

**When:** Before work begins
**Who:** Team Lead + Contributor
**Storage:** Team Lead, Contributor, Commons copy

## Quick Reference

**Onboarding new member:**
1. Send Membership Agreement
2. Share Privacy Policy
3. Provide Code of Conduct
4. Create pseudonym + SHADOW ID
5. Invite to project (if applicable)

**Starting new project:**
1. Complete Project Charter (Team Lead)
2. Recruit team
3. Collect Contributor Agreements
4. Store copies with Commons
5. Begin work

**Project dispute:**
1. Attempt direct resolution
2. File Dispute Complaint with Commons
3. Commons schedules arbitration hearing (within 10 days)
4. Arbitration panel issues decision (within 30 days)
5. Implementation and appeal period

**Emergency:**
1. Notify Commons immediately
2. Commons activates emergency authority (72 hours max)
3. Provide full investigation within 24 hours
4. Member Assembly reviews within 7 days

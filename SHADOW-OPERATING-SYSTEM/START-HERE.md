## Key Concepts (TL;DR)

| Concept | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| **SHADOW** | Decentralised organisation where members use pseudonyms for internal work; external clients verify member credentials without seeing legal identity |
| **Pseudonym** | Permanent identifier (e.g., "Zenith") used internally; portable across projects; builds reputation over time |
| **Team Leader** | Person who creates a project; has operational authority over that project only |
| **Commons** | Minimal central layer providing infrastructure, arbitration, and optional services (e.g., revenue apps); takes 0% by default |
| **Project Sovereignty** | Each project operates independently; Team Leader decides internal processes |
| **Member Assembly** | All members vote on major decisions (if 15+ members) |
| **Arbitration** | 5-level conflict resolution (direct negotiation → arbitrator → assembly appeal → external legal) |

---

## What's Different About SHADOW

✅ **Anonymity** — Work under pseudonym internally  
✅ **Decentralised** — No central authority; decisions at lowest level  
✅ **Voluntary** — No automatic labor obligations; clear agreements  
✅ **Sovereign Projects** — Each project makes own decisions  
✅ **Resilient** — Survives loss of founder, infrastructure failure, etc.  
✅ **Scalable** — Works at 5 members and 10,000+ members  
✅ **Fair** — Revenue stays with project/members; Commons takes 0% by default

## Before You Start

### ⚠️ You Should Know
- SHADOW requires clear documentation (decisions, agreements, etc.)
- Coordination requires trust (choose members carefully)
- Pseudonymity requires discipline (protect real identities)
- Legal entity is required (consult counsel for your jurisdiction)
- Tax compliance is your responsibility (not SHADOW's)

### 🛡️ What SHADOW Protects
- Member privacy (encrypted identity storage)
- Project autonomy (no central veto)
- Fair compensation (team keeps revenue by default)
- Decision rights (clear authority matrix)
- Dispute resolution (neutral arbitration)

### ⚠️ What SHADOW Doesn't Protect
- Bad project management (that's Team Leader's responsibility)
- Interpersonal conflicts (arbitration is neutral, not protective)
- Legal liability (consult counsel)
- Tax evasion (pay what's required by law)
- Harassment (has Code of Conduct + enforcement)

## Common Questions

**Q: Is SHADOW a legal entity?**  
A: No. SHADOW is a governance model. It requires a legal entity (company, coop, etc.) chosen for your jurisdiction.

**Q: Can I use SHADOW today?**  
A: Yes. Everything is ready to deploy. Start with QUICK-START.md.

**Q: What if I have questions?**  
A: Refer to specific sections in the documentation. Most answers are in the relevant Part (I–XIV).

**Q: How long does implementation take?**  
A: Phase 1 (proof of concept): 4–8 weeks. Full rollout (100+ members): 12–24 months.

**Q: Do I need to be a programmer?**  
A: No. Recommend tech-savvy person for infrastructure, but most of SHADOW is process/governance.

**Q: What's the cost?**  
A: Legal (£2–5k), infrastructure (£200–500/month), insurance (£1–2k/year). Covered by founding budget or Commons revenue apps.

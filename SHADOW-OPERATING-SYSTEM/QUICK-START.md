## Step 2: Establish Legal Foundation (Week 2)

**Time:** 3–5 days

### Actions

1. **Choose jurisdiction**
   - See Part VII (Legal & Institutional) for recommendations
   - UK: Consider Community Interest Company (CIC) or Cooperative
   - US: Consider Delaware Public Benefit Corporation
   - Other: Consult local counsel

2. **Engage legal counsel** (cost: £2–5k for incorporation)
   - Provide: `SHADOW Charter template` from Part XIII
   - Ask: "Can this jurisdiction support anonymous members?"
   - Outcome: Draft SHADOW Charter

3. **Secure funding for legal/infrastructure** (target: £5–10k)
   - Commons operating costs (servers, legal, administration)
   - Should NOT be contingent on member recruitment
   - See Part VI (Economic System) for financial model

### Deliverable

✅ **Incorporated legal entity** with draft Charter

---

## Step 3: Onboard Founding Members (Weeks 3–4)

**Time:** 2–3 weeks

### Actions

1. **Recruit 3–5 trusted founding members**
   - Each should understand the full model
   - Each should be willing to put projects into SHADOW
   - Each should be willing to be members for 6+ months

2. **Run founding member onboarding**
   - Have each read Part I (Foundational Architecture) + Part IV (Membership)
   - Run 1-hour discussion per person on model & their commitment
   - Document their pseudonym choice and agreed commitment (template in Part XIII)

3. **Establish first arbitrator**
   - One founding member should agree to serve as arbitrator
   - Read Part III (Section 6: Arbitration)
   - Be trained on conflict resolution procedures

4. **Record founding membership**
   - Document each member in internal records (encrypted)
   - Issue each their SHADOW pseudonym
   - Create member directory (encrypted, access-controlled)

### Deliverable

✅ **3–5 verified founding members**
✅ **Arbitration authority designated**

---

## Step 4: Launch & Verify (Month 2)

**Time:** 2–4 weeks

### Actions

1. **Adopt SHADOW Charter**
   - Have all founding members sign/accept
   - File with legal entity
   - Publish summary (anonymised) to signal legitimacy

2. **Establish communications**
   - Secure chat system (see Part XII: Digital Architecture)
   - Member directory (encrypted)
   - Project management system (see Part XII)

3. **Launch first project**
   - One founding member originates a project
   - Use Project Charter template (Part XIII)
   - Recruit 2–3 additional members (can be external, anonymous)
   - Run for 4–8 weeks

4. **Document and verify**
   - Keep meticulous records (required by later audit)
   - Record all decisions, agreements, transactions
   - Verify project completion and member satisfaction

### Deliverable

✅ **SHADOW Charter signed & filed**
✅ **First project completed**
✅ **Proven model works** (at 5–8 member scale)

## Month 3 & Beyond: Scale

Once you've proven the model works (Months 1–2), begin recruiting:

- Target: 15–30 members by end of Month 6
- Launch 3–8 additional projects in parallel
- Test arbitration system (if needed)
- Begin collecting revenue-share (optional)

See Part XIV (Implementation Roadmap) for full 24-month plan.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

🚫 **Skipping legal foundation** — Do this first. Ambiguity causes massive problems later.

🚫 **Overcomplicating early governance** — Use "Stage 1" governance (direct consensus, no formal voting) until you have 15+ members.

🚫 **Not documenting decisions** — Keep a decision log from day 1. You'll need it.

🚫 **Recruiting members too fast** — Better to have 5 excellent members than 20 confused ones.

🚫 **Assuming everyone understands the model** — Run onboarding for every new member. Spend 2 hours explaining.

🚫 **Centralising infrastructure control** — Distribute credentials from day 1. No single person should have all access.
